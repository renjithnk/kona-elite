Our New "KonaElite Espresso" Product

Introducing our newest addition of our exquisite KonaElite coffee family. The invention of the coffee capsule has revolutionized coffee drinking, fuelling the increasing popularity of one of the most distinctive beverages available. Our small but strong black KonaElite coffee known as the espresso. KonaElite coffee is brewed in many ways, but the espresso is fast becoming the connoisseur's cup of choice. An exquisite KonaElite espresso coffee is brwed under great pressure by forcing hot water through finely ground beans in less than 25 seconds. The result is a unique concentrated shot of Kona coffee crowned with its hallmark, delicate, chocolate-brown froth known as a crema. Come experience our new expansion of our KonaElite coffee family tree.
